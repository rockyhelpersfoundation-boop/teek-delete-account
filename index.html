Delete Your Account

You can delete your Teek account and all associated data at any time. No questions asked.

Last updated: November 2026
⚠

30-Day Grace Period

When you request deletion, your account is scheduled for deletion in 30 days. If you log back into the app within that window, the deletion is cancelled and your account is fully restored. After 30 days, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1

Delete From Inside the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the Teek app:

  1. Open the Teek app and sign in
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top-right corner
  3. Select Delete Account from the menu
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation field
  5. Enter your account password
  6. Tap Delete My Account

You will be signed out immediately. Your account will be scheduled for deletion in 30 days.

2

What Gets Deleted

When your 30-day grace period ends, the following is permanently deleted:

  • Your profile — name, username, email, phone number, bio, photos
  • All posts, articles, and stories you created
  • All comments you wrote on any post
  • All likes, bookmarks, and shares you made
  • Your followers list and everyone you followed
  • All direct messages sent and received
  • All notifications
  • Your referral code and referral history
  • Your user category, bio, and social links
  • All preferences and settings
3

What We Keep (For Legal Compliance)

Some records must be retained to comply with financial and legal regulations:

  • Transaction records — anonymized (name, email, and other identifiers removed) and kept for 5 years to satisfy tax and financial reporting laws
  • Abuse and moderation logs — kept for 90 days in case of an ongoing safety investigation

These records cannot be used to identify you after anonymization. They contain no personal data.

4

What Happens Immediately

When you tap Delete My Account:

  • You are signed out of all devices
  • Your profile becomes invisible to other users
  • Your posts are no longer visible in any feed
  • Your messages are hidden
  • Nobody can follow you
  • Your account status changes to "scheduled for deletion"

All of this is reversible during the 30-day window — just log back in and the deletion is cancelled.

5

What Happens After 30 Days

After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted from our database. This is irreversible:

  • Your profile cannot be recovered
  • Your posts, comments, likes, and followers cannot be recovered
  • Your email and phone number are released — you may register a new account with the same details
  • Your referral code is retired
6

Don't Have the App Installed?

If you no longer have the Teek app installed and cannot delete your account from inside the app, email us:

Email: support@teek.com

Send your request from the email address registered to your account. Use the subject line "Delete my account". We will process your request within 7 days and reply with confirmation.

7
